Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to strategic questions about my SEO methodologies, performance track record, and technical capabilities.

I am an SEO Executive & AEO Specialist holding an MBA in Digital Marketing. My expertise lies in Technical SEO, Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), and search intent alignment, backed by over a year of experience managing high-traffic international domains.

The 300% growth in impressions and 100%+ click increase was driven by four key pillars:

  • Content Depth: Expanded thin pages from 300 to 1,500+ intent-focused words.
  • Core Web Vitals: Reduced LCP page load times from 4.2s to 2.1s.
  • Schema Markup: Injected structured FAQPage, Article, and BreadcrumbList schemas.
  • Internal Linking: Designed semantic topic clusters to maximize link equity distribution.

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the process of structuring website data so conversational AI engines (like Google's new RAG-powered AI Overviews, Perplexity, and ChatGPT) can easily parse and cite it. Following the latest Google I/O 2026 Search Box upgrades, search behavior is shifting rapidly from keyword links to dynamic conversational answers.

I align websites with Google's new "Optimizing for Generative AI Features" guidelines by:

  • E-E-A-T Enrichment: Creating first-person, experience-driven content to bypass AI "commodity content" filters.
  • Semantic Structure: Using clean code, descriptive headings, and structured data so RAG systems can accurately pull site citations.
  • Indexation Audits: Resolving crawl blockages to ensure the traditional search index (which powers Google's AI models) has full site access.

To optimize crawl budget, indexation, and Core Web Vitals (LCP, INP, CLS), I use a professional SEO stack:

  • Diagnostics: Google Search Console and Screaming Frog to resolve crawl and indexation errors.
  • Search Intelligence: Ahrefs and Semrush for competitor analysis and keyword mapping.
  • Performance: Chrome DevTools and PageSpeed Insights for frontend optimization.

I construct semantic topic clusters that build topical authority:

  • Pillar-Cluster Model: Grouping highly related subtopics around high-value parent pages.
  • Contextual Anchors: Utilizing descriptive anchor text to pass authority (PageRank) to key landing pages.
  • Crawl Efficiency: Reducing click depth to ensure search bots index core pages within 3 clicks.
