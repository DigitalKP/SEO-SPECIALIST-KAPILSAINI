Key Learnings

  1. Authority compounds when relevance is controlled: focused backlinks created clearer trust signals than random volume-heavy campaigns.
  2. AEO works better with trust: structured answers perform more strongly when the domain itself shows better authority indicators.
  3. Proof matters in SEO storytelling: before-and-after snapshots make the case study more believable and portfolio-ready.
  4. Steady growth beats noisy spikes: a clean authority lift is easier to explain and more valuable for long-term organic positioning.
