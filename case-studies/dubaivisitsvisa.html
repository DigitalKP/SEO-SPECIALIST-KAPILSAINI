The Challenge

Situation: DubaiVisitVisa.com was struggling with low organic visibility in the highly competitive UAE visa niche. Despite having functional content, the site was failing to capture significant search traffic due to poor on-page optimization, thin content, and lack of structured data implementation.
  • Low click-through rates from SERPs
  • Content not aligned with search intent
  • Missing schema markup for rich snippets
  • Poor internal linking structure
  • High bounce rate from organic traffic

Strategy & Execution

  • Content Audit & Restructure

    Analyzed top 50 pages using GSC data. Restructured content to match search intent, expanded thin pages from 300 to 1500+ words with FAQ sections.

  • On-Page Optimization Blitz

    Rewrote all title tags and meta descriptions with CTR-focused copy. Implemented proper H1-H6 hierarchy with keyword variations.

  • Schema Markup Implementation

    Added FAQPage schema to 20+ high-traffic pages, Article schema to blog posts, and BreadcrumbList for better entity recognition.

  • Internal Linking Architecture

    Built topic clusters around visa types, creating 200+ contextual internal links to distribute link equity.

Timeline & Milestones

Month 1: Foundation

Complete technical audit, fixed 150+ crawl errors, implemented Core Web Vitals improvements (LCP reduced from 4.2s to 2.1s).

Month 2: Content Overhaul

Restructured 30 priority pages, added FAQ sections targeting long-tail keywords, implemented FAQ schema.

Month 3: Optimization

Built internal linking network, optimized for featured snippets, refined meta tags based on CTR data.

Month 4: Results

Achieved 3x impression growth, 100%+ daily clicks, 8 pages in Top 10 rankings for competitive terms.
