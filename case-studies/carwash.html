The Challenge

Situation: Bubble Brothers Car Wash, a local car detailing provider, was struggling to get visibility in local Google Searches. Their Google Business Profile (GBP) was buried on page 2 or 3 of Google Maps, and their website was not optimized for high-intent geo-targeted keywords, causing them to lose massive business to local competitors.
  • Poor visibility in local map pack search queries
  • Inconsistent business Name, Address, and Phone (NAP) information across local directories
  • Lack of location-specific service landing pages
  • Zero structured Schema Markup to establish local entity connection for search engines
  • Low online review velocity and lack of Google review optimizations

Strategy & Execution

  • Local Entity Schema & Semantic Linking

    Developed and injected custom JSON-LD LocalBusiness schema. Utilized Google Maps coordinates, offer catalogs, and nested service coordinates to verify the business entity location to Google's semantic database.

  • Google Business Profile (GBP) Refinement

    Optimized business categories (primary & secondary), descriptions, photo metadata (geo-tagging), and structured the product/service catalog to match regional queries.

  • Hyper-local Content Silos

    Created service-area landing pages targeting "[Service] in [City Name]". Wrote search-intent aligned copies with localized FAQs to target AI search snippet boxes.

  • NAP Alignment & Citation Auditing

    Conducted a full citation sweep. Corrected duplicate and incorrect listings across 50+ authority directories (Yelp, Apple Maps, YellowPages) to build consistent domain trust.

Timeline & Milestones

Month 1: Citation Clean-up & Audit

Fixed 35+ NAP citation errors, completed Google Business Profile category restructuring, and optimized site loading speeds for mobile devices.

Month 2: Entity Schema & Geo-pages

Designed and deployed location-specific service pages. Deployed nested geo-coordinate schema markup to align service areas in search indexing.

Month 3: Review Velocity & Growth

Implemented automated SMS feedback systems to boost review collection, resulting in a consistent rise from Rank #14 to Top 3 in Google Maps searches.
